ChatGPT can write you a sonnet. It cannot tell your customer where their parcel is.

Month two starts here, and it starts with the obvious fix.

The confusion is between fluency and knowledge. A general model has read the internet. It has never read your store. Those are different qualifications and only one of them is on the job description.

What it has: language, reasoning, tone, confidence. Genuinely excellent, all four.

What it lacks: your catalogue, your stock levels, your policies, their order. All four are the job.

There is no wire between the model and your store. A shopper asks about a real product, the model has no catalogue feed, no inventory, no order lookup, and it answers anyway, because answering is the only thing it knows how to do.

The result is beautifully written and completely useless. "Our merino crew is a customer favourite and typically restocks every few weeks, do check back soon." Three sentences, no stock check, no date, no product, nobody helped.

And a wrong answer is not neutral. It is a promise made in your name, in your brand voice, on your website. No answer costs you one sale. A confident wrong answer costs you the sale, the refund, and the review.

Here is the test to run on anything. Ask it something only your store could know. Not "what is your returns policy". Ask whether a specific product is in stock in a specific size. Whatever comes back tells you which kind of system you are looking at.
