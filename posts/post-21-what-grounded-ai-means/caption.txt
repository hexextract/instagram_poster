Grounded AI is the difference between an answer and a guess.

If you learn one AI concept as a store owner, make it this one.

Grounding means retrieve before you speak. The system is not allowed to answer from memory. It has to go and fetch a real fact from your real store first, and the answer has to be built out of what it fetched.

Understand the question. Retrieve the fact. Answer from the fact, not from what stores like yours usually say.

Same question, two sources of truth:

Ungrounded: "Our merino crew usually restocks every few weeks, do check back soon."

Grounded: "The merino crew in medium is out of stock. The large is available now, and the medium is due back on the 14th."

One of those was written from a pattern. The other was written from your inventory.

What it should be grounded in is a short list: your product catalogue, your inventory, your published policies, and the order in front of it. If an answer is not in one of those four, there is no answer to give.

Which is the second half of grounding, and the half that gets skipped. It has to be able to say nothing. Grounding is only half a safeguard if the system can still improvise when retrieval comes back empty.

Grounding is what makes an answer yours. Everything else in an AI support tool is preference. This one is structural, because it decides whether the thing speaking in your brand voice is quoting your store or improvising near it.

Save this, it is the one that matters.

One question for any vendor: what is it grounded in, and what happens when it finds nothing?
