Deflection rate measures how well you avoided your customers.

It is the only metric in support that improves when your service gets worse.

Somebody asks a question, gets a menu, tries again, gets the menu again, gives up and closes the tab. Deflected. The number goes up. Somewhere a dashboard turns green.

Resolution is the honest number. Did the person get what they needed, first time, without a second message?

The two can move in opposite directions and frequently do. A store with 80% deflection and a rising repeat contact rate has not automated its support, it has hidden its inbox.

We are stating this now, in week 8, before we have anything to sell you, because the alternative is stating it later and having it read as positioning.

What does your team report, deflection or resolution?
