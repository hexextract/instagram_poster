Twenty minutes and a spreadsheet will tell you more than any support dashboard.

You need your last fifty tickets and nothing else.

Step 1. Pull the last fifty. Not a clean sample, not last month. The last fifty in order, including the weekend ones and the ones you would rather not reread. Fifty is enough for the pattern to appear and small enough to finish in one sitting. A curated sample only tells you what you already believe.

Step 2. Tag each one by type. Eight tags maximum: sizing, delivery date, WISMO, stock, returns, damage, order edit, other. If you need a ninth you are describing the customer instead of the question.

Step 3. Mark which were pre purchase. One extra column, yes or no. Did this person already own the product, or were they deciding whether to buy it?

Step 4. Count the repeats. How many of the fifty are the same question in different words?

Typical result: fifty tickets, eight to twelve distinct questions underneath them, and a third or more of the whole list is pre purchase.

What you end up with is your own evidence rather than ours. Every argument this account has made for seven weeks either shows up in your fifty tickets or it does not, and that is a far stronger position than believing us.

One condition: do not skip step three. Everybody tags the types and stops. The pre purchase column is where the uncomfortable number lives and it is the whole reason for doing this.

Save it, run it this week.
