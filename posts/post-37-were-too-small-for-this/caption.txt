"We're too small for AI support." You're exactly the size that pays the most for not having it.

Here is the thing about repetition: somebody always absorbs it. The only question is who.

A 200 person team absorbs it as a rota. It has a budget line, somebody is paid for it, and it ends at 6pm. A cost, absorbed.

A team of one absorbs it out of their own working day. No budget line, nobody paid for it, and it never ends. A cost, personally.

Same forty questions. Completely different price, because your hour is the scarcest thing in the company and it is the only input the business genuinely cannot replace.

Which is why the threshold is not about order volume. Three questions:

Do the same questions come up most days?

Do they arrive outside your working hours?

Do they arrive in languages you do not speak?

Three yeses and volume is irrelevant.

Small stores need this more, not less. A large team can put three people on live chat and be genuinely excellent. A team of one cannot be awake at 2am, cannot answer in Japanese, and cannot be in two places at once when a drop goes live.

And the honest version, because the pitch is worthless without it. If you get four questions a week and answer them happily over coffee, none of this applies to you and we would rather say so than sell you something.
