Support is not a cost centre. It is the last step of your checkout, and the only one you have never optimised.

Think about how the rest of the funnel gets treated.

You will A/B test a product page for a month. You will rewrite the cart copy twice. You will argue about button colour with a straight face and you will be right to, because those things move numbers.

Then somebody reaches the last step, has one final objection, sends it to you, and waits nine hours.

That is the same funnel. That is the same customer. It is just the one step where the response time is measured in business days and nobody has ever run an experiment on it.

Support gets budgeted as a cost because it is measured as a cost. Ticket volume, handle time, cost per contact. Every one of those metrics assumes the goal is to spend less on it.

Measure it as a step in the checkout instead and the questions change completely. How many people asked something before buying? How many bought? How long did they wait?

Nobody has that dashboard. Everybody has the other one.
