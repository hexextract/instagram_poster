Here's exactly what our AI can see in your store. And what it can't.

This is the last question people ask before installing, so we are answering it publicly rather than one at a time in DMs.

What it reads, all read only:

Products, variants, prices and descriptions.

Inventory levels.

Published policies and store pages.

Order status, for the customer asking about their own order.

What it never touches:

Payment details or card data.

Your customer list, in bulk.

Write access to products or orders.

Anything in your Shopify admin settings.

The word doing the work there is scoped. When a customer asks about their order it can see that order. It cannot browse your order history, export a customer list, or answer a question about somebody else's purchase.

And the important one: it has no write access to anything. It cannot change a price, edit an order, alter stock, issue a refund or publish a page. Every scope it holds is read only, which means the worst case is a bad answer and never an altered store.

Leaving is the normal Shopify path. Uninstall from your admin, access is revoked immediately, and store data is deleted on request and by default on a defined schedule.
