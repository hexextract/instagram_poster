Thirteen weeks, forty nine posts, one argument. Here it is on one screen.

Weeks 1 to 4, the problem. Half your tickets are sales questions wearing a support costume. Five revenue leaks live in your inbox and none of them appear in Shopify Analytics. Eight questions, forty times a day, costing six hours of typing and roughly a day of lost depth.

Weeks 1 to 4, the customer. Nobody waits for your reply, they open another tab. An hour now reads as indifference, because the baseline was set by everything else in their phone. WISMO is not asking where, it is asking whether.

Weeks 5 and 6, the obvious fix and why it fails. Fluency is not knowledge, and a general model has never read your store. A confident wrong answer is worse than none, because it is a promise you now own. Grounding is the whole distinction: retrieve before you speak, or do not speak.

Weeks 7 and 8, the standard, published before we sold anything. Five parts to a good answer, and answer second. An AI must be able to say "I do not know". Four things it must never handle: anger, disputed money, damage, ambiguity.

Weeks 9 and 10, the mechanism. Read, retrieve, answer, hand off. Three seconds, broken down. Four things it will never do, in writing.

Weeks 11 to 13, the arithmetic, stated conservatively. Repetition alone, no invented percentages. Read only, no write access, ever.

The whole thing in one line: support is the last unoptimised step of your checkout, and it is the only one you can fix this week.
