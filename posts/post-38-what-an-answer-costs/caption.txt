An hour of your time versus twelve cents.

That is the whole comparison, and it is deliberately the only cost figure we publish.

The framing most vendors use is AI against your support team, which invites you to imagine replacing people and makes everyone uncomfortable, us included. It is also the wrong comparison for a store the size of most stores, because there is no team to replace. There is you, at 9pm, answering a sizing question.

So the real comparison is an answered question against a founder's hour.

About twelve cents per resolved query. Whatever an hour of building your business is worth, and only you know that number.

We are not going to tell you the trade is obviously good, because for some stores it will not be. Four questions a week and the maths does not work. Two hundred and it does, comfortably.

No invented savings percentages here, and no "stores like yours typically see" figures. Just the one number you can check.

Do the maths for your store, then tell us the number.
